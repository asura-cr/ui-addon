## Features

### 1. Gribble Limiter
![Gribble Limiter](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/797a6f0b-4bef-4f5a-b0a2-7b00b01f22fb =500x250)
Only displays the Slash and Power Slash buttons on Gribble pages.

---

### 2. Attack till x dmg
![Attack till x dmg](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f7d84424-4702-4ca5-8bc5-51580ff2c75d =500x250)
Attack up to a specified damage limit, capping at your desired value.

---

### 3. Hit All Gribbles for 1M Damage
![Hit all gribbles](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2499b26a-0044-4672-b1b4-5bc44c9216c3 =500x250)
Deals 1 million damage to all Gribbles on the screen.

---

### 4. Add Custom Amount of Stat
![Add Custom Stat](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4cd3e1e6-8536-4109-9499-7390ba8c19c5 =500x250)
Customize and adjust specific stats as per your preference.

---

### 5. View All D2 Minibosses in One Page
![D2 Minibosses](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fd4f1477-253c-4a21-ba02-98cde79ce9db =500x250)
View all D2 minibosses listed on a single page for easy access.
